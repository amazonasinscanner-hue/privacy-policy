Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 20, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Amazon Variation Scanner Pro ("we", "our", or "the Extension") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Chrome browser extension.

Summary: We collect minimal data necessary to operate the extension. We do not sell your data to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Email Address: When you sign up for a free trial or purchase a subscription, we collect your email address to manage your account and subscription status.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Extension Settings: Your preferences (such as auto-scan settings) are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API.
  • License Status: We verify your subscription status to determine access to premium features.

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect or store Amazon product data you scan
  • We do NOT track your browsing history
  • We do NOT collect personal information beyond your email
  • We do NOT access your Amazon account credentials

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Managing your subscription and trial period
  • Providing access to premium features
  • Sending important account-related notifications
  • Improving the extension's functionality

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

3.1 ExtensionPay

We use ExtensionPay to manage subscriptions and payments. When you subscribe, ExtensionPay processes your email address to manage your subscription status. For more information, visit extensionpay.com.

3.2 Stripe

Payment processing is handled by Stripe. Your payment information is sent directly to Stripe and is not stored on our servers. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Extension settings are stored locally in your browser
  • Subscription data is stored securely by ExtensionPay
  • Payment data is stored securely by Stripe
  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information

5. Data Retention

We retain your email address and subscription information for as long as you maintain an active account. If you cancel your subscription, your data may be retained for up to 12 months for accounting and legal purposes, after which it will be deleted.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Cancellation: Cancel your subscription at any time

7. Children's Privacy

Our extension is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
