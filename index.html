Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Amazon Variation Scanner ("we", "our", or "the Extension") handles your information. Our goal is to provide a powerful tool while respecting your privacy to the fullest extent.

Summary: Amazon Variation Scanner is now 100% free. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data, financial information, or browsing history.

1. Information We Collect

We have designed the Extension to operate without requiring any personal information from you.

1.1 Local Data Only

  • Extension Settings: Your preferences (such as auto-scan settings) are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. This data never leaves your device.

1.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect your name, email address, or any other contact information.
  • No Financial Data: Since the extension is free, we do not collect any payment or billing information.
  • No Tracking: We do not track your browsing history or use any analytics to monitor your activity.
  • No Product Data Storage: We do not store or transmit the Amazon product data you scan; all processing happens locally in your browser.

2. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not use your information for any marketing, profiling, or third-party sharing. The local settings are used solely to provide the functionality you expect from the Extension.

3. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not communicate with any third-party servers for tracking or data collection. It only communicates directly with Amazon.com to retrieve the product information you request during a scan.

4. Data Security

All data used by the extension remains on your local machine. We rely on the security features of the Google Chrome browser to protect your locally stored settings.

5. Your Rights

Since we do not store any of your data on our servers, you have full control over your information. You can delete all extension data at any time by simply uninstalling the Extension or clearing your browser's local storage.

6. Children's Privacy

Our extension is not intended for children under 13 years of age. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect or store data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Given our commitment to zero data collection, any future changes will only further clarify or strengthen these protections.
